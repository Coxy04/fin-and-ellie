Good to know

Questions

What should I wear?

Smart. Suits, dresses, jumpsuits. The ceremony is indoors but the drinks are outside if the weather allows, so bring something for your shoulders. Grass and gravel, so heels at your own risk.

Can I bring a plus one or children?

Your invitation lists everyone who's invited. If you're not sure, just ask, we'd rather you did.

What about dietary requirements?

Tell us when you RSVP and we'll pass everything to the caterers.

Can I request a song?

Yes, that's part of the RSVP. One song each that gets you on the dance floor. We can't promise the DJ will play all of them.

Is there parking?

Yes, free and on site. You can leave your car overnight.

Will the pug be there?

Of course. He has a bow tie.

Can I take photos?

Please do, but we'd love the ceremony itself to be phone-free. We have a photographer for that bit and we'd rather see your faces than your screens.

When do I need to reply by?

1 March 2027. You can change your answer any time before then by coming back to this page.

Reply by 1 March 2027
Will you join us?

Let us know who's coming, what you can't eat and the one song that will get you on the dance floor.
